Frequently Asked Questions • Chandigarh Taxi Booking

How do I book a cab with NorthRide Cabs in Chandigarh?

You can book directly using our online booking form above or call our 24x7 customer support directly at +91 98723 47745 / +91 99887 58566. You will immediately receive a Booking Reference and Customer ID.

What is the one-way cab fare from Chandigarh to Delhi?

Our comfortable AC Sedan starting fare for Chandigarh to Delhi is ₹3,199 with transparent per-km billing and no hidden charges.

Are cabs available for late night and early morning airport drops?

Yes, NorthRide Cabs operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. We recommend scheduling your airport cab at least 2 hours in advance for seamless pickup.

Which car categories are available for booking?

We provide well-maintained Sedans (Dzire, Etios), premium SUVs (Innova Crysta, Ertiga), MUVs, and luxury vehicles for weddings, corporate events, and family vacations.
